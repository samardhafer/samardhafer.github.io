VeriTruth

Developed an AI-powered misinformation and deepfake detection application designed to help users verify digital content and identify manipulated media. The project focused on creating a trustworthy and accessible verification experience by combining content analysis, deepfake detection, and AI-generated insights within a single platform.

My Role

  • Researched misinformation, deepfakes, and digital verification challenges.
  • Analyzed existing fact-checking and verification tools to identify gaps and opportunities.
  • Developed the project concept, features, and overall product strategy.
  • Created user personas, user journeys, and verification workflows.
  • Designed the information architecture and feature structure.
  • Planned and designed the complete UX/UI experience in Figma.
  • Built wireframes, user flows, and high-fidelity interface designs.
  • Defined the platform's unique value proposition and user-focused functionality.
  • Presented the final concept, prototype, and design solution.

Tools Used: Figma, Canva, Google AI Studio, ChatGPT, Miro, UX Research Methods

Key Skills: UX/UI Design, Product Design, User Research, Information Architecture, Wireframing, Prototyping, Design Thinking, AI-Assisted Design, Problem Solving, Digital Verification Systems, Creative Direction, User Experience Strategy
