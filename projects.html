In Between: Nowhere & Everywhere

Developed and produced a podcast exploring themes of identity, belonging, cultural transitions, and the experience of living between different places and cultures. The project focused on personal storytelling and reflective conversations about feeling connected to multiple communities while not fully belonging to any one of them.

My Role

  • Researched topics related to identity, culture shock, and personal experiences.
  • Developed the podcast concept, title, and episode themes.
  • Planned interview questions and episode structure.
  • Recorded and edited podcast content.
  • Created branding, promotional materials, and social media content.
  • Wrote episode descriptions and audience engagement content.
  • Managed the overall creative direction and storytelling approach.

Tools Used: Adobe Audition, Canva, Spotify for Podcasters

Key Skills: Podcast Production, Storytelling, Content Creation, Audio Editing, Research, Interview Planning, Branding, Creative Direction

Solo academic project

VeriTruth

VeriTruth is an AI-powered misinformation and deepfake detection app designed to help users verify digital content. I developed the project from the initial concept and brainstorming stage, conducting research on misinformation challenges and analyzing existing verification tools to identify market gaps. I designed the user experience by creating user flows, feature structures, and verification workflows, then developed the UI in Figma with a focus on simplicity, accessibility, and trust. What makes VeriTruth unique is its ability to combine content verification, deepfake detection, and transparent AI-driven insights within a single user-friendly platform.
